## Term Project Overview: Cat 6 Routing & Length Estimator
In my Systems Analysis & Design course this semester, I am leading the design of a **Cat 6 Cable Routing & Length Estimator** application. 

This application addresses a key operational challenge in structured cabling projects: accurately predicting physical cable lengths and determining optimal routing paths through complex floor plans before installation begins. The system calculates precise 3D distances (including horizontal runs and vertical wall drops), automatically enforces physical constraints (such as avoiding high-voltage power lines and adhering to standard bend radii), and applies standard industry buffers for service loops and slack. Furthermore, it incorporates automatic compliance verification to alert designers whenever a run exceeds the 100-meter Ethernet performance ceiling. 

By automating these spatial calculations, the project eliminates manual estimation errors, reduces expensive material waste, and delivers executive-ready material estimates and cost breakdowns for network infrastructure planning.
